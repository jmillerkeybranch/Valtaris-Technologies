MIDAS

SMS Authorization & Security Alerts

Operated by Jason Miller

MIDAS is a software application operated by Jason Miller. Jason Miller uses SMS to deliver transactional notifications to authorized MIDAS users who voluntarily choose to receive them. Messages may include authorization requests, security notifications, account or service alerts, and other communications the user has requested or authorized.

Optional SMS enrollment

SMS enrollment is optional. You may use MIDAS and its services without consenting to receive SMS messages. If you choose to receive SMS messages, enter your mobile phone number and actively check the consent box below. Leaving the box unchecked means you are declining SMS enrollment.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance. See the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

About the SMS program

Messages are intended for the person who affirmatively opts in. SMS enrollment is voluntary and separate from access to MIDAS and its services. We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers, mobile information, or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency varies based on activity and requests. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.
